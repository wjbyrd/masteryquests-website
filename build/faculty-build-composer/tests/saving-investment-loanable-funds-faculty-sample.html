SLF1 · easy · easyBoss

Which action is saving in the macroeconomic sense?

  1. Setting aside current income for future use (key)
  2. Buying newly produced factory equipment
  3. Building a newly constructed apartment
  4. Adding unsold goods to business inventories

Feedback: Saving is current income not used for consumption; the other choices are forms of new macroeconomic investment.

  1. Y − T − C (key)

  1. A firm buys newly produced machinery (key)
  2. A household buys an existing corporate share
  3. An investor buys an existing government bond
