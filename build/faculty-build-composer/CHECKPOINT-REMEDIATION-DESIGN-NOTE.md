# Checkpoint Remediation Invariant

Standard Campaign checkpoints are gates: three correct answers are required. The first wrong boss answer immediately applies the original three-room setback, bounded by the last successfully cleared checkpoint. A failed attempt awards no artifact or boss defeat. Repair, Bridge, and Recovery Retest do not intercept this setback. Returning to the checkpoint starts a fresh attempt.

Other modes retain their existing diagnostic/assessment checkpoint sequences. Each checkpoint delivers its three selected questions without leaving the checkpoint room. Every response is scored and recorded in adaptive memory, telemetry, and Mastery Report evidence. A miss advances to the next checkpoint question; it does not launch Repair, Bridge, or Recovery Retest.

After the checkpoint summary resolves, ordinary adaptive routing resumes. The existing remediation thresholds may launch a consolidated remediation sequence from the post-checkpoint room when subsequent eligible evidence satisfies them. Its return room is therefore the post-checkpoint room, never the completed checkpoint.

This invariant does not alter checkpoint objective selection, cross-concept fallback, pool structure, question count, remediation thresholds, or remediation content.
