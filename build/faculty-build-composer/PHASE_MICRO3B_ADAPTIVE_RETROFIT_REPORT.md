# Phase Micro3b — Adaptive Retrofit Closure

## Scope

Retrofitted the already-granular Elasticity, Consumer/Producer Surplus, and International Trade families after the faculty composer exposed thin adaptive coverage at the child-concept level.

This phase does **not** reopen the taxonomy and does not expand Legendary, checkpoint, calculation, or graph scope. It adds only measured ordinary adaptive depth and Repair/Bridge support.

## Locked adaptive standards

Two different coverage tests are now tracked.

1. **Runtime adaptive depth**
   - Standalone/focused child: at least 10 Easy, 10 Medium, and 10 Hard questions after calculation/integration questions are routed to their canonical difficulty.
   - Supporting child: at least 5 Easy, 5 Medium, and 5 Hard questions. Supporting children are intended to be paired with adjacent concepts.

2. **Adaptive support**
   - Standalone/focused child: at least 6 Repair and 6 Bridge questions.
   - Supporting child: at least 3 Repair and 3 Bridge questions.

The Easy/Medium/Hard floor protects Exam/Timed-style adaptive stages from immediate recycling. The Repair/Bridge floor is the composer-facing "Adaptive support" standard and gives the remediation router multiple viable recovery paths.
