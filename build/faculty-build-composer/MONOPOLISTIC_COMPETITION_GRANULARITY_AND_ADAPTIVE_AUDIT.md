# Monopolistic Competition Granularity + Adaptive Coverage Audit

## Verdict

**READY FOR FIVE-SUBTOPIC GRANULARIZATION WITH A SMALL, TARGETED ADAPTIVE RETROFIT.**

The current Monopolistic Competition family is healthy. It contains **412 canonical questions**, passes the existing five-mode composer validation, retains **55 graph-linked questions across 38 assets**, and does not need a broad content rebuild.

The problem is the same one exposed in the previous Micro families: once the parent is divided into instructor-facing subtopics, some lanes inherit plenty of Legendary/checkpoint material but too little ordinary Easy/Medium/Hard depth or Repair/Bridge support.

The clean instructor-facing taxonomy is **five subtopics**.

## Why five, not six

The inherited bank contains six objectives, but `MCMP.3` and `MCMP.4` form one coherent causal sequence:

**short-run profit/loss → entry/exit → firm demand shifts → long-run zero profit/tangency → markup and excess capacity.**

Splitting entry/exit from long-run equilibrium would create a sixth selector, but it would divide a process instructors normally teach as one connected adjustment story. Combining those objectives produces a deep, coherent **178-question** lane without sacrificing useful granularity.

The other four objectives remain pedagogically distinct enough to deserve their own selectors.

## Adaptive standards applied

The locked Micro granularity standard is used here:

- **Standalone/focused child:** minimum **10 Easy + 10 Medium + 10 Hard**, plus **6 Repair + 6 Bridge**.
- **Supporting child:** minimum **5 Easy + 5 Medium + 5 Hard**, plus **3 Repair + 3 Bridge**.
- Future Quiz viability: at least **15 ordinary non-Legendary questions** after any required adaptive-depth fill.

Calculation questions count toward their canonical runtime difficulty because that is how the composer actually routes them.

## Subtopic findings

### 1. Market Structure & Product Differentiation — Supporting

This lane covers the definition of monopolistic competition, many firms, differentiated products, close substitutes, downward-sloping firm demand, and physical/location/service/brand differentiation.

The content is appropriate as a targeted selector, but it is intentionally introductory. It currently has strong Easy coverage and almost no Medium/Hard spine. It needs **4 Medium + 5 Hard + 1 Repair = 10 additions**.

After the fill it will also clear the future 15-question Quiz threshold.

### 2. Short-Run Firm Choice, Profit/Loss & Shutdown — Standalone

This lane is already extremely healthy. It includes MR = MC output choice, reading price from demand, profit/loss calculations, shutdown, and integrated short-run graph analysis.

Runtime adaptive depth is already **12 / 11 / 34**. The only gap is support routing: **+1 Repair and +3 Bridge**.

No ordinary question expansion is needed.

### 3. Entry, Exit & Long-Run Equilibrium — Standalone

This is the strongest lane in the bank. It includes entry after profit, exit after loss, shifts in each existing firm's demand, zero economic profit, long-run tangency, markup, allocative/productive inefficiency, and excess capacity.

It already sits at **15 / 22 / 23** runtime Easy/Medium/Hard with **8 Repair / 7 Bridge**.

**No additions needed.** Leave it alone.

### 4. Advertising, Branding & Nonprice Competition — Standalone

This is the only genuinely thin standalone lane. It contains advertising as information or persuasion, advertising demand shifts, selling costs, quality competition, brand signals, innovation, and other nonprice competition.

The bank has **53 total records**, but the ordinary adaptive spine is only **1 / 4 / 8** with **3 Repair / 3 Bridge**. Advanced material masks the weakness.

Required fill: **9 Easy + 6 Medium + 2 Hard + 3 Repair + 3 Bridge = 23 additions**.

The topic already has 18 quiz-eligible ordinary questions, so this is not quiz filler. The fill is specifically for repeated adaptive use and Unlimited practice.

### 5. Product Variety, Efficiency & Model Limits — Supporting

This lane covers variety benefits, welfare tradeoffs, allocative inefficiency, market classification, and limitations of the monopolistic-competition benchmark.

It is appropriately smaller and should remain supporting. Current runtime coverage is **1 / 1 / 4** with **2 Repair / 3 Bridge**.

Required fill: **4 Easy + 4 Medium + 1 Hard + 1 Repair = 10 additions**.

That fill also raises ordinary quiz-eligible material above 15 without inventing a larger standalone campaign role for the topic.
