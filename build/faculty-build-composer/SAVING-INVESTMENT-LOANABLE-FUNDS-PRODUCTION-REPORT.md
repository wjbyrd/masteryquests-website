## 16. Saving-accounting convention

All accounting uses explicit textbook net-tax notation: private saving is `Y − T − C`, public saving is `T − G`, and T means net taxes/net tax revenue net of transfers. National saving is private plus public saving and equivalently `Y − C − G` in the stated setup. The bank never substitutes total federal revenue minus total federal outlays for public saving.

## 17. Closed-economy S = I safeguards

Assertions of `S = I` are qualified as aggregate closed-economy identities. Questions explicitly reject the individual-saver misconception and the claim that domestic saving must equal domestic investment in every open economy. The focused regression passed.

## 18. Movement-versus-shift safeguards

Real-interest-rate changes are described as movements along saving supply and investment demand. Non-interest determinants shift curves. The focused validator searches stems, keys, and feedback for prohibited higher-rate-causes-shift assertions while allowing an explicitly identified misconception; it passed with zero violations.

## 19. Saving-shift coverage

The bank covers household willingness to save, saving incentives, public saving, deficits, surpluses, causal direction, reverse inference, and graph-based equilibrium changes for both supply directions.

## 20. Investment-demand coverage

Technology, expected future demand, expected profitability, investment tax incentives, and weaker expectations are used to shift desired investment. Higher financing rates remain movements along demand.

## 21. Deficit/crowding-out coverage

The full chain is pinned: deficit → public saving down → national saving down → loanable-funds supply left → real interest rate up → private investment down. The bank owns this market mechanism and does not recreate multiplier/aggregate-demand calculations.

## 22. Simultaneous-shift coverage

Questions distinguish determinate quantity results from rate ambiguity when supply and demand shift together, identify the unambiguous variable for opposite-direction shifts, and use LOANABLE-08's visible magnitudes rather than manufacturing ambiguity.

## 23. Phase 3/open-economy boundary

Domestic saving/investment gaps, international capital flows, balance-of-payments accounting, and exchange rates are excluded and remain reserved for later roadmap phases.
