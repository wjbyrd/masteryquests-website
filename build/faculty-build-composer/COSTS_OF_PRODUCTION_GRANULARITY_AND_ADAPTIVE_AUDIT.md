# Costs of Production — Granularity + Adaptive Coverage Audit

## Verdict

The authoritative Costs of Production bank contains **442 canonical records** and is healthy at the family level. The problem is the same one exposed by the earlier Micro families: several distinct instructional ideas are packed into one selector, and the existing ordinary/adaptive-support distribution is uneven once those ideas are separated.

The cleanest taxonomy is **10 child subtopics**. This stays inside the expected 10–12 range without creating tiny artificial selectors.

## Why ten instead of forcing twelve

Two combinations are deliberate.

**Marginal Cost & Production–Cost Linkages** keeps marginal-cost calculation/interpretation together with the MP→MC relationship. Splitting those would create two thin selectors that instructors generally teach as one causal story.

**Short-Run Cost Curves: Relationships & Shifts** keeps MC/AVC/ATC relationship questions together with fixed-cost, variable-input-cost, and productivity comparative statics. Average-cost measures and marginal cost remain independently selectable, so this child is specifically the curve-interaction/comparative-statics layer rather than another generic “costs” bucket.

This avoids taxonomy for taxonomy’s sake.

## Supporting topics

Three children should be treated like Income and Cross-Price Elasticity rather than forced to carry a full campaign alone:

- **Economic Costs: Explicit, Implicit & Opportunity Cost** — best paired with Profit Concepts.
- **Sunk & Avoidable Costs** — best paired with Short-Run Cost Curves or decision/application material.
- **Minimum Efficient Scale & Constant Returns to Scale** — best paired with LRAC and economies/diseconomies of scale.

They remain useful granular selectors, but their natural instructional footprint is smaller.

## Adaptive backfill audit

Using the Micro standard established during the Elasticity/Surplus/Trade retrofit:

- Standalone/focused child target: **10 Easy + 10 Medium + 10 Hard**, plus **6 Repair + 6 Bridge**.
- Supporting child target: **5 Easy + 5 Medium + 5 Hard**, plus **3 Repair + 3 Bridge**.

The Costs family is heavily weighted toward Elite, Legendary, boss, and integrated material. That is why the family looks enormous while some granular adaptive lanes are still thin.

If these ten children are locked exactly as proposed, the measured maximum backfill is:

- **102 ordinary Easy/Medium/Hard questions**
- **64 Repair/Bridge adaptive-support questions**
- **166 targeted additions total**

That would move the Costs family from **442 to 608** records. Nothing would be added to Legendary, checkpoint, calculation, or graph inventories unless a separate defect is found.

## Quiz implications

All seven standalone/focused children already have at least 18 ordinary non-Legendary quiz-eligible questions. The three supporting children are currently smaller (6, 5, and 6), which matches their recommended paired status.

If the proposed adaptive-depth backfill is completed, each supporting child would also reach at least 15 ordinary questions, giving the future Quiz mode flexibility without pretending the topic should carry a full adaptive campaign alone.

## Recommendation

Lock these **10** Costs of Production child concepts, then perform the same sequence used for the previous Micro families:

1. Apply child taxonomy metadata without duplicating physical banks.
2. Add only the measured 166-question adaptive retrofit.
3. Preserve every existing 442 question record unchanged.
4. Recompute child Repair/Bridge route maps.
5. Validate exact parent/child recombination.
6. Validate standalone/focused Timed + Exam operation and paired-support operation.
7. Preserve the parent Costs family as an all-content selector.

No other Costs expansion is justified by this audit.
