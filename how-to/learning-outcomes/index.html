Learning Outcome Coverage

What can you choose to cover in a Mastery Quest?

Start with a concept, then choose the learning outcomes you teach. Explore the available outcomes below before building your game.

Open the ComposerRead the Composer guide

From concepts to assessable coverage

Concept → Learning outcomes → Question coverage

Each concept offers one or more broad instructional outcomes. Students show this performance by selecting answers, interpreting graphs, calculating values, classifying examples, or applying economic reasoning. The labels do not imply that students write explanations.

Outcomes are broader than individual question skills and may combine several related subskills. You can choose coverage without inspecting individual questions. A selected outcome makes its supporting questions available; it does not guarantee that every outcome will be sampled in a short run.

Choose a preset or make it your own

Brief
Essential learning outcomes for the concept.
Standard
Normal/default coverage for the concept.
Full
All available learning outcomes.
Custom
A different combination selected manually.

Some concepts have the same Standard and Full coverage because there is no distinct broader extension layer. If a manual selection matches a preset, the Composer can show that preset name. New concept selections start with Standard coverage.

One outcome can be the right amount

Some concepts stay together as one coherent outcome because the current question bank does not support a defensible finer split. This is intentional and helps preserve useful question coverage. Brief, Standard, and Full then coincide.

Check readiness after choosing outcomes and game modes. Narrow selections and supporting concepts may need related content to support a particular mode.
