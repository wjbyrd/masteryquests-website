Faculty Resources

Request Faculty Question Banks

Instructors may request the complete Composer question banks for instructional review, customization, and course development. The General Economics, Microeconomics, and Macroeconomics collections are available in PDF and CSV formats. Some questions are shared across collections.

These faculty materials include correct answers and instructor-facing information. They are supplied privately after manual verification of faculty eligibility; an institutional email address alone does not authorize access. Please do not repost the banks publicly.

Request by email

Email wjbyrd@troy.edu with your name, institution, institutional email, and the collection or collections you need. You may add a brief description of your intended instructional use. The creator will review your request and arrange private delivery if approved.

No mailing-list subscription is created by requesting materials. Do not include student records or sensitive personal information.
