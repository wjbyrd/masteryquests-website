The analysis runs the production compose() function for all 86 visible Micro
topic cards and all 79 visible Macro topic cards, using FULL content scope,
automatic checkpoint selection, and all ten modes. It also tests the 12 Micro
and 14 Macro navigation-family bundles and each whole-course selection.
Shared General topics appear in both areas where the application includes them.
Parent topics and derived child topics overlap. Do not sum all row deficits
as a count of unique questions to create. Narrower outcome selections and custom
checkpoint restrictions are outside this audit and may need more coverage.

Both whole-course selections support all ten modes. Of individual topic cards,
12 Micro and 4 Macro support all ten. All observed mode failures are shortages,
with no question-validation issues or other composition errors.

Files:
- mode-coverage.csv: current counts and all deficits for topics, families, courses.
- mode-specific-gaps.csv: exact current count, requirement and shortfall per mode.
- authoring-packages.csv: alternative additions, ranked by modes unlocked per
  question. Packages are independent options evaluated against today's bank,
  not cumulative tasks. Larger packages may subsume smaller ones.

Pool vocabulary and minimums:
- Quiz: 5 ordinary Easy, 5 Medium, 5 Hard.
- Timed Trial / Unlimited Practice: 6 of each, 1 Repair, 1 Bridge.
- Standard Campaign / Exam Drill / Score Attack: the preceding counts plus
  3 Checkpoint One (easyBoss), 3 Checkpoint Two (mediumBoss), and
  3 Final Checkpoint (finalBoss) questions.
- Legendary: 6 ordinary Legendary and 3 Legendary checkpoint (legendaryBoss).
- Trial by Graph: 10 ordinary graph-dependent questions. A graph image and
  graphRequired flag must be present, with valid accessibility metadata.
- Fading Fortune: 10 ordinary questions with exactly four options.
- Risk & Reward: 10 ordinary questions.

Checkpoint stages and cognitive difficulty are distinct. Adding ordinary Easy
questions cannot fill an easyBoss shortage. Elite alone has no separate mode
minimum, although ordinary Elite questions can count for Graph, Fading Fortune,
and Risk & Reward. A graph located only in a checkpoint does not qualify for
Trial by Graph.

Authoring package projections are arithmetic eligibility estimates. They assume
new, unique, valid, correctly routed questions and four answer options for new
ordinary questions. Graph package tier choices remain open; correctly chosen
tiers could resolve additional gaps beyond the conservative projection. The
same question can meet an ordinary-tier need and a graph requirement. Re-run
composition after authoring to verify actual eligibility and scope routing.

Integrated Economic Analysis is intentionally designed to pair with foundational
concepts. Its solo gaps are labeled integration-only and should not become a
blanket authoring mandate. The hidden Advanced Macro Checkpoint Supplement is
not audited as a standalone topic. It is included in the whole-Macro check.

   of GDP, Living Standards and Growth, Money Functions and Measures; two each
   for CPI versus GDP Deflator, Foreign-Exchange Market Equilibrium, Real
   Exchange Rates and Purchasing Power. Each topic gains five or six modes.
2. Micro checkpoint additions: one Checkpoint One each for Short-Run Cost Curves
   and Total Surplus; two for Applications of Elasticity; one at each of the
   three stages for Price Elasticity of Supply. These unlock Campaign, Exam,
   and Score Attack for those focused topics.
3. Substantial focused coverage: Macro LRAS/Potential Output has 2 Easy, 1 Medium,
   3 Hard and supports no mode alone. Micro Market Power has 2 Easy, 2 Medium,
   1 Hard, no Bridge and supports no mode alone. They need balanced coverage.
4. Ordinary Legendary gaps: Consumer Choice, Factor Markets, Inequality/Poverty,
   and Information Asymmetry/Behavioral/Political Economy each need six. Two
   ordinary Legendary questions unlock the combined Macro Money/Banking/Fed
   family. One each unlocks individual PED and Liquidity Preference topics.
5. Graph authoring: the combined Macro GDP, Inflation Measurement/Real Values,
   Growth/Productivity, Unemployment/Labor, Money/Banking/Fed, and Money Growth/
   Inflation families each lack all ten graph-dependent questions. Favor
   interpretation of meaningful economic graphs rather than decorative images.
   Micro LRAC needs only one; Cost Components, Efficient Quantity, and Price
   Elasticity of Supply each need two to unlock Trial by Graph.

Minimums establish availability, not sufficient variety for repeated play.
