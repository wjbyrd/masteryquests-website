About the model & your progress

A simplified competitive market with linear demand and supply. Curve-shift missions check direction and equilibrium consistency; the size of a shift is illustrative. Price-control missions specify exact values and assume no government purchases. Prices are dollars per crate; quantities are crates per market day.

Each run has five missions and a short recall quiz. Progress stays in this browser. No accounts, timers, remote data collection, or LMS grade transfer. Completion is practice evidence, not a full mastery assessment.
