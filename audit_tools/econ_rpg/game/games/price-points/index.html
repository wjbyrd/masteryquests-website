About the models & your progress

Price Points uses illustrative demand and supply functions. Own-price changes move along curves; related prices and income shift demand while own price stays fixed. Percentage comparisons use midpoint elasticity. The straight-demand revenue challenge also identifies point elasticity.

Revenue means gross receipts, not profit or welfare. Horizontal and vertical curves are idealized limiting cases. Curve shapes alone do not identify every real-world elasticity.

Progress stays in this browser. No accounts, analytics, or LMS grade transfer. Download your notebook to keep your observations. Completion after feedback is practice evidence, not a full mastery assessment.
