Money in Motion

About this model

Operate a simplified textbook reserve-requirement model. These fictional rules are not a complete description of modern banking.

Banking Terms
Deposits
Money banks owe depositors / customer account balances.
Reserves
Funds the bank holds for settlement and reserve needs in this simplified model.
Loans
Amounts borrowers owe the bank.
Required reserves
The minimum reserves implied by the fictional reserve ratio.
Excess reserves
Reserves above that minimum.

Required and excess are two measures of the same reserve account, not separate accounts.
