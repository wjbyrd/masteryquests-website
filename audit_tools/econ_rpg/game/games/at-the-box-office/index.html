HOW TO PLAY

  • Each week begins with its own reference figures. Outcome changes compare with that reference.
  • Make one market decision for the week.
  • See the resulting attendance and receipts.
  • Market-reading points reflect how well your decision fits that week’s stated goal and the available evidence.
  • Ticket receipts are revenue, not profit; costs are not modeled.
  • The progress track follows your seven-week run.
