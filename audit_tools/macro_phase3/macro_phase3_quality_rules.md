# Principles Macro Phase 3 Quality Rules

These rules identify candidates for individual human review; they do not authorize automatic rewriting.

## Remove internal routing and game labels

Do not expose labels such as “Boss Audit,” “Repair check,” “Bridge check,” named-boss narration, or similar generation and routing language. Ask the economic question directly while preserving Boss, Checkpoint, Repair, Bridge, and Adaptive routing metadata.

## Remove curriculum-map and sequence language

Questions should ask the economic relationship directly rather than refer to a “neighboring concept,” “later concept,” “next topic,” “which bridge,” or an internal family boundary such as F2/F6.

## Remove generated filler from complete options

Delete suffixes such as “as the relevant economic conclusion,” “in the situation described,” and “for the decision being evaluated” when they add no substantive economic condition.

## Require item-specific, mechanism-based feedback

Feedback must explain why the keyed answer follows in the item’s actual scenario. Generic formulas, concept lists, institution-tracing instructions, or neighboring-process boilerplate are insufficient when the question tests a specific transaction, policy chain, labor-market mechanism, productivity calculation, or inflation cost.

## Use professional instructional prose

Remove colloquial or evaluative asides such as “nasty,” “ugly,” “brutal,” “not magical,” “not glamorous,” or “wreck the textbook story.” Explain the economic tradeoff or mechanism directly.

## Keep answer-option grammar and units complete

Read options as continuations of the stem. Pronouns and verbs must agree with the subject, keyed options must be complete economic statements, and numerical labor-surplus answers must state worker units when the stem asks how many workers are affected.

## Reconcile every calculation with its scope and feedback

State whether a calculation concerns one production stage or the entire domestic production chain. Exact-Fisher, productivity-growth, GDP, inflation, and labor-market feedback must show the same method and result as the keyed answer.

## Preserve correct causal direction in policy chains

When borrowing puts upward pressure on real interest rates, the standard crowding-out channel puts downward pressure on private investment. Monetary- and fiscal-policy feedback should trace the complete tested chain rather than state only the final aggregate-demand result.

## Treat GDP transactions consistently

Tips paid for a service must be treated consistently as expenditure and income. For multi-transaction GDP items, feedback should classify each relevant transaction and reconcile the keyed total without implying that imports mechanically reduce domestic production.

## Recover missing graph evidence from authoritative assets

When a question depends on a missing image, authorize recovery of the verified source asset or a confirmed equivalent. Do not invent graph values, leak graph-derived answers into the stem, or weaken the graph-assessment contract.

## Preserve keyed position and unlisted metadata

When wording or an option must change, preserve the keyed answer position/mapping unless the proposal explicitly states otherwise. Preserve difficulty, objective, skill, routing, graph metadata, accessibility metadata, and assets unless named in Change Scope or Preserve / Notes.
