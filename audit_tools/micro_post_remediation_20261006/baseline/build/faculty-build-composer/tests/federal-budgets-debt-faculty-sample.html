43060 · FB1: Federal Budget Accounting

Question: When federal outlays exceed federal revenues during a fiscal year, what is the result?

  1. A budget deficit
  2. A budget surplus
  3. A balanced budget
  4. A reduction in gross debt

Feedback: A deficit occurs because annual outlays are greater than annual revenues.

  1. Deficit: annual flow; debt: accumulated stock
  2. Deficit and debt: both annual fiscal flows
  3. Deficit and debt: both accumulated fiscal stocks
