Competitive Efficiency & Model Limits
THE CORE IDEA
Perfect competition is allocatively efficient when price equals marginal cost and productively efficient when
firms produce at minimum average total cost. The benchmark requires private and social marginal costs to
align. Apply the exact efficiency conditions and distinguish behavioral assumptions from welfare
assumptions. Minimum average total cost represents the least-cost scale for producing that output in the
benchmark.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
Apply the exact efficiency conditions and distinguish behavioral assumptions from welfare assumptions.
•
Minimum average total cost represents the least-cost scale for producing that output in the benchmark.
•
A representative competitive firm is in long-run equilibrium at the minimum point of ATC.
•
Price reflects marginal willingness to pay while MC reflects marginal opportunity cost.
WATCH OUT
A common mistake is to equate efficiency with equity. Apply the exact efficiency conditions and
distinguish behavioral assumptions from welfare assumptions. Minimum average total cost represents
the least-cost scale for producing that output in the benchmark.
