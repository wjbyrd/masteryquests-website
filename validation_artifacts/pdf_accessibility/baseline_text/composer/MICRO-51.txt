THE CORE IDEA
Dynamic Strategy: Repeated Games, Credibility & Entry Deterrence examines repeated interaction,
credibility, commitments, limit pricing, entry deterrence, continuation incentives, and sequential strategic
reasoning. A higher continuation factor increases the weight on future consequences. Cooperation PV is
12.50; cheating path PV is 16.25. Net gain = 25 − 8 − 14 = 3 million. Aggressive competition and predation
must be distinguished with evidence.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
Determine whether firms interact once or expect to meet again.
•
Compare the immediate gain from cheating or entering with future losses from retaliation.
•
A threat affects behavior only when carrying it out would be credible.
•
Longer relationships, patience, and observable actions make cooperative outcomes easier to sustain.
WATCH OUT
Do not assume every low price is predatory. Net gain = 25 − 8 − 14 = 3 million. Aggressive competition
and predation must be distinguished with evidence.

The game tree is solved backward. If entry occurs,
the incumbent receives 7 by accommodating and 3 by
fighting, so accommodation is the credible response
