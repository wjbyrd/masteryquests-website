Short-Run Firm Choice, Profit/Loss & Shutdown
THE CORE IDEA
Short-Run Firm Choice, Profit/Loss & Shutdown uses MR = MC, firm demand, ATC, and AVC to determine
output, price, profit or loss, and short-run shutdown. The firm shuts down when price cannot cover variable
cost. At the chosen output, price above ATC means profit, price below ATC means loss, and equality means
zero economic profit.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
Use marginal revenue and rising marginal cost to choose the firm's output.
•
Read price and average total cost at that output to determine profit or loss.
•
Use average variable cost to determine whether the firm operates or shuts down in the short run.
•
Keep output choice, profit measurement, and shutdown analysis as three distinct steps.
WATCH OUT
A common mistake is to misapply monopolistic competition rule. At the chosen output, price above ATC
means profit, price below ATC means loss, and equality means zero economic profit.
WORKED EXAMPLE: SHORT-RUN FIRM DECISION
The firm chooses 36 units where MR = MC at $15,
then charges $27 on its demand curve. ATC is also
$27, so total revenue and total cost are both $27 x 36
= $972. Economic profit is zero, but P > MC shows
the outcome is not allocatively efficient.
