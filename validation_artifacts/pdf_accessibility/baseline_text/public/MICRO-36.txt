Monopoly Power, Barriers to Entry & Sources of Monopoly
THE CORE IDEA
A monopoly has durable market power because barriers prevent close rivals from entering and eroding that
power. Barriers can keep profit from triggering the entry response that erodes profit in competitive markets.
Natural monopoly can make one-firm production cost-efficient while still creating a market-power problem.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
A city has one coffee shop today, but entry is easy and several rivals are preparing to open.
•
A software firm is the only seller this year.
•
The current one-seller market does not by itself establish durable monopoly power.
•
One seller becomes a durable monopoly only when entry is meaningfully blocked.
WATCH OUT
Do not treat any large firm as monopoly. Durable monopoly power depends on the relevant market and
barriers to entry. A monopoly requires meaningful barriers to entry, not merely one seller at a moment in
time.
