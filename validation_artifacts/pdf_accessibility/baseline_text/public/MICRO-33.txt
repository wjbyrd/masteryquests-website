Entry, Exit & Long-Run Equilibrium
THE CORE IDEA
Entry and exit move a competitive industry toward long-run equilibrium with zero economic profit. More firms
supply more total output at each price. At long-run equilibrium the firm maximizes profit and entry/exit has
eliminated economic profit. Entry and exit remove persistent economic profit or loss in the benchmark model.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
At long-run equilibrium the firm maximizes profit and entry/exit has eliminated economic profit.
•
Entry and exit remove persistent economic profit or loss in the benchmark model.
•
Long-run industry assessment 10: Demand falls in a constant-cost industry and some firms leave.
•
Long-run industry assessment 11: Demand rises in a constant-cost industry and entry is unrestricted.
WATCH OUT
Do not miss long run equilibrium conditions. At long-run equilibrium the firm maximizes profit and
entry/exit has eliminated economic profit. Entry and exit remove persistent economic profit or loss in the
benchmark model.
WORKED EXAMPLE: ENTRY AND ZERO ECONOMIC PROFIT
The market equilibrium price is $25. At that price, the
representative firm produces 50 units where P = MC,
and $25 is also the minimum ATC. Revenue covers
all explicit and implicit costs, so economic profit is
zero and no entry or exit pressure remains.
