Market Equilibrium
THE CORE IDEA
Market equilibrium occurs where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. At that price, buyers can
purchase the amount they want and sellers can sell the amount they offer, so there is no pressure for price to
change. A demand or supply shift creates a new intersection and can change both equilibrium price and
equilibrium quantity.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
Find the price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.
•
On a graph, use the intersection of the demand and supply curves.
•
A price below equilibrium creates a shortage; a price above equilibrium creates a surplus.
•
Report both the equilibrium price and the corresponding equilibrium quantity.
WATCH OUT
A common mistake is to call any intersection or stated price an equilibrium. Equilibrium occurs only
where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.
