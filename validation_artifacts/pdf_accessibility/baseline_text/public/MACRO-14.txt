Unemployment Measurement
THE CORE IDEA
Labor-force status depends on work, job attachment, availability, and active job search. Employed people
include those working for pay or profit, those temporarily absent from a job, and qualifying unpaid family
workers. Unemployed people have no job, are available, and actively searched in the past four weeks;
people awaiting recall from temporary layoff need not search. Discouraged workers who have stopped
searching are outside the labor force.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
Work for pay or profit during the reference week counts as employment, even if part-time.
•
At least 15 hours of unpaid work in a family business also counts as employment.
•
Labor force = employed + unemployed; unemployment rate = unemployed / labor force x 100.
•
Participation rate = labor force / civilian noninstitutional population age 16 and older x 100.
WATCH OUT
Wanting a job alone does not make a person unemployed. Check job status, availability, and active
search, including the temporary-layoff exception. Discouraged workers are outside the labor force.
WORKED EXAMPLE: CLASSIFYING LABOR-FORCE STATUS
Among 100 people in the civilian noninstitutional population age 16 and older, 60 are employed, 5 are
unemployed, and 35 are outside the labor force. Labor force = 60 + 5 = 65. Unemployment rate = 5/65 x
100 = 7.7%; participation rate = 65/100 x 100 = 65%.
