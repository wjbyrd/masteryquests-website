Monopoly Profit, Loss & Shutdown
THE CORE IDEA
Monopoly profit or loss is measured after the profit-maximizing output and demand-based price have been
found. The firm must cover avoidable variable cost. Output optimization and profit measurement are separate
steps in monopoly analysis. Profit or loss equals (P−ATC)Q at the chosen output.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
Choose monopoly output where rising marginal cost equals marginal revenue, then read price from
demand.
•
At that output, compare price with average total cost to determine profit or loss.
•
Compare price with average variable cost to decide whether a loss-making monopolist operates in the
short run.
•
Measure profit or loss as the price-cost gap multiplied by output.
WATCH OUT
A common mistake is to mix output choice with profit measurement. Output optimization and profit
measurement are separate steps in monopoly analysis. Profit or loss equals (P−ATC)Q at the chosen
output.
WORKED EXAMPLE: MONOPOLY PROFIT OR LOSS
The firm chooses 36 units where MR equals MC. At
that output, demand gives a price of $42 and ATC is
$27. Profit per unit is $42 - $27 = $15, so total
economic profit is $15 x 36 = $540.
