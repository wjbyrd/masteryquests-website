Income Elasticity of Demand
THE CORE IDEA
Income elasticity of demand measures how demand changes when consumer income changes. Income
elasticity helps firms anticipate demand shifts as consumer income changes. Different income responses
shift the composition of demand as purchasing power changes. Normal goods have positive income elasticity
because demand moves with income. A small percentage demand response relative to income means an
income elasticity near zero.
HOW TO RECOGNIZE IT
•
Demand for restaurant meals rises when consumer income rises.
•
A small percentage demand response relative to income means an income elasticity near zero.
•
Household income rises and demand for bus rides falls.
•
A retailer estimates income elasticity for its store brand at -0.6.
•
Demand for a normal good rises as income rises.
WATCH OUT
A common mistake is to misread the sign of income elasticity. A positive coefficient identifies a normal
good; a negative coefficient identifies an inferior good.
WORKED EXAMPLE: CALCULATING INCOME ELASTICITY
Household income rises by 10%, and demand for restaurant meals rises by 15%. Income elasticity is
15% / 10% = 1.5. The positive sign identifies restaurant meals as a normal good, and the coefficient
above 1 indicates that demand responds more than proportionally to income in this example.
