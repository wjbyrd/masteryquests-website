## Boundary

The live preset and resolved records, not the exam, define scope. All listed production/cost, competition, monopoly/regulation and core game-theory mechanisms are selected. First- and third-degree discrimination are selected; second-degree taxonomy is not. Incumbent demand loss and consumer variety benefits are selected, but named business-stealing/product-variety externalities are not current taxonomy. Antitrust rationale, collusion evidence and a predation checkpoint are selected. Tying, resale-price maintenance, Sherman/Clayton statute identity and private treble-damages standing are absent and will not be imported.

## Calibration

Read the complete final, all tables and all eight embedded images. Q1–25 calibrate Hard application; Q26–30 calibrate Elite/Legendary integration. The exam’s profit-payoff matrix does not establish total social welfare. The exam’s fixed-input production schedule does not by itself establish long-run MES. Preserve economic reasoning without inheriting those ambiguities or its distinctive settings/data.
