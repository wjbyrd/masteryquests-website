## 17. Economic and numerical defects corrected

43205 no longer describes S=I as merely an equilibrium relationship; 43231 explicitly separates always-valid realized accounting, including inventories, from planned imbalances. 43203 and 43210 now distinguish changes in schedules at a given rate from final realized saving, avoiding a false fixed-quantity inference in simultaneous-shift scenarios.

43098 defines gross taxes, transfers, purchases and interest before comparing public saving with the full budget. 43134 states the borrowing-finance assumption before connecting a deficit to the debt stock. The graph reconstructions give an explicit currency scale when dollar magnitudes matter and derive shift sizes from the actual straight lines. 43162 removes an unstable dataset “last updated” claim while retaining its dated-stock interpretation. No reversed graph key or image-byte defect was found in this pool.

The February 2026 CBO figures remain explicitly historical projections: revenues/outlays of 5.6/7.4 trillion, a separately rounded 1.9 trillion deficit, debt ratios of 101/120 percent, and rising interest outlays. The report itself warns that rounded components may not sum to totals; retained rounded-value questions are not erroneous for that reason. New ratio exercises use displayed values and label their conditional calculations. [CBO February 2026 baseline](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/62105).

Debt measures and dated stock observations retain their distinct Treasury meanings. No live debt amount was substituted into the bank. Treasury’s official reporting pages distinguish debt held by the public, intragovernmental holdings and debt-limit measures, and provide dated debt history. [Treasury debt reporting](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/government/public-debt-reports/debt-position-and-activity-report/), [Treasury debt history](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/mobile/).

## 23. Remaining limitations

This is a content and delivery audit, not an empirical item-discrimination or student-performance study. Some retained direct upper-tier questions, similar introductory grammars and easy-to-eliminate alternatives remain. Adequate Hard applications were preserved rather than rewritten for stylistic uniformity.
