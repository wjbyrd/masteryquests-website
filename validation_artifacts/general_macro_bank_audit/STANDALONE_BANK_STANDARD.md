# Standalone Bank Standard

## Why the production minimum is not the standalone standard

The composer/runtime minimum is a safety floor: Easy 6, Medium 6, Hard 6, Elite 4, Legendary 6, each ordinary boss tier 3, Legendary Boss 3, Repair 1, Bridge 1. Standard and Score use 9 ordinary rooms at each of Easy/Medium/Hard and three questions at each checkpoint. Exam uses a 10/10/10 map. Timed uses the same map but may end before room 30. Legendary uses 27 ordinary Legendary rooms and three checkpoints that all draw from the same Legendary Boss pool.

The runtime suppresses only a recent window and can fall back when fewer than three candidates remain. Therefore minimum-size pools can pass preflight while recycling questions inside one run. The targets below set a compact lower bound at one-run uniqueness plus limited rotation; upper bounds add replay and alternate misconception coverage. Counts are pool-slot targets, not normalized difficulty totals. A calculation item intentionally cross-listed in an ordinary pool can satisfy both coverage goals without duplicate authoring, so the final unique-canonical total may sit slightly below the displayed pool-slot sum.

## Interpretation

- **Foundational conceptual:** emphasize household, time, resource, business-choice, and policy tradeoff scenarios. Six Repair items should span distinct misconceptions; definitions belong mostly here and in Bridge.
- **Market / graphical:** provide graph-reading, shift-versus-movement, comparative-statics, and verbal-to-graph transfer. Eight calculations are a flexible target; do not add arithmetic where the concept is inherently qualitative.
- **Calculation-heavy:** cover setup, units, interpretation, reverse calculation, and misleading-data traps—not number swaps. Calculation items supplement rather than replace reasoning pools.
- **Macro policy:** represent tools, transmission lags, constraints, short-run/long-run effects, competing goals, and unintended consequences. Include numerical work only where the policy concept naturally uses it.
- **Macro measurement / conceptual:** combine classification, data interpretation, measurement limitations, and unfamiliar cases. The lower calculation target can be zero when the assigned concept is nonnumerical.
- **Integrated macro / transfer:** prioritize linked causal sequences and policy/shock combinations. Easy is retained only to meet supported-mode preflight; Integration and Bridge are larger because transfer is the concept's purpose.

## Readiness rubric

- **ROBUST:** comfortably exceeds a complete run's unique selections and has broad Repair/Bridge coverage.
- **ADEQUATE:** covers a complete run with little or no forced recycling but limited replay padding.
- **THIN:** passes engine preflight but is below complete-run or remediation-breadth expectations.
- **NOT STANDALONE READY:** fails at least one required engine pool for the mode.

## Quality constraints on expansion

Targets are ceilings only when content remains high quality. Do not fill a pool with trivial name/number swaps. Main pools should be application-led; pure definitions should normally diagnose or reconstruct knowledge in Repair/Bridge. Bosses need multiple skills and scenario forms, not three rephrasings. Legendary items should be truly multistep or transfer-oriented because the runtime uses them for 27 rooms.
