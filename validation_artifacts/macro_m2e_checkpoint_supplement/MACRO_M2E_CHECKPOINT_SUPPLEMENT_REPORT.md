## Runtime contract

- The supplement contributes **no ordinary Easy/Medium/Hard practice** and does not dilute selected concept banks.
- It cannot compose by itself; at least one normal concept is required.
- Eligible challenges are filtered by selected prerequisite concepts.
- Standard and Score Attack checkpoints may replace **only question 3** with at most one eligible challenge.
- Legendary checkpoints use the same one-question replacement with opening/middle/final stage preference.
- Timed Trial and Exam Drill do not invoke the supplement.
- If no eligible challenge matches the checkpoint focus, the normal third boss question remains in place.
- Missed challenges carry a normal concept remediation destination. Runtime Repair, Bridge, and retest selection explicitly prefers that underlying concept rather than the inactive legacy integrated Repair/Bridge records.
