1. **M2 policy and structural floor batch:** finalize role candidates; decide whether best-paired supporting slices must launch all five modes solo; repair F10 preflight first; preserve hashes until authoring is approved.
2. **M2 measurement/growth/labor batch:** F1–F4, prioritizing thin ordinary/checkpoint pools and calculation diversity.
3. **M2 money/policy batch:** F5–F7, emphasizing Fed/Tools boundaries, Fisher, same-skill Repair, and Money Market→Transmission Bridge.
4. **M2 stabilization batch:** F8–F10, with Long-Run Adjustment and the full Phillips progression as the critical path.
5. **M3 synthesis/quality batch:** F11 combination diversity, graph accessibility/ambiguity remediation, semantic duplicate review, and definition-heavy F5 modernization.
6. **M3 verification:** deterministic static checks, then bounded family representation probes; large-session simulation remains a later phase.
