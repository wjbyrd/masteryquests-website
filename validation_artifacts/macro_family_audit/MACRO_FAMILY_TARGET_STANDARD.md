# Macro Family Target Standard

## Governing philosophy

A mature Macro product is a mature **chapter-sized family with meaningful granular representation**, not 42 isolated 100-question banks. Granular IDs remain available for Composer selection, diagnostics, Repair, Bridge, skill analysis, and faculty customization.

## Mature family

A family is mature when it:

- passes every pedagogically applicable mode preflight;
- supplies at least one clean unique run where that is the family’s intended mode (10 per Easy/Medium/Hard for Exam/Timed, 27 Legendary and 9 Legendary Boss for Legendary);
- has at least nine records in each ordinary boss tier, distributed meaningfully across siblings rather than captured by one concept;
- is application-led and rotates scenarios, skills, calculations, and graphs;
- has same-skill Repair for each major recurring misconception and explicit Bridge routes across the family sequence;
- prevents a large sibling bank from starving smaller selected concepts. A cold-start share below roughly 55% of equal share, or a boss/Legendary share below roughly 45–50% of equal share, triggers review; exact equality is not required.

## Healthy supporting granular concept

A supporting slice normally needs current launch floors—6 Easy, 6 Medium, 6 Hard, 6 Legendary, 3 per ordinary boss tier, 3 Legendary Boss, at least 1 Repair and 1 Bridge—only when faculty are expected to launch it alone in all modes. If its production role is explicitly best-paired, M2 may instead document/guard solo modes, but it must still receive meaningful family representation and diagnostic routes. Do not expand to 27 Legendary merely to avoid all solo reuse.

## Standalone depth

Standalone depth is justified for a central prerequisite, a substantially taught topic, a concept with many authentic task families, or a frequent faculty solo selection. Standalone does not mean 100 by default. It means launch-safe pools, strong applications, rotated bosses, robust Repair, and no early or immediate repetition.

## Controlled reuse

Controlled reuse is acceptable after every eligible record in the target pool has appeared once and the recent-window suppression prevents short loops. For a supporting solo Legendary bank, 10–14 high-quality records plus 4–6 Legendary Boss records is a reasonable target; mixed family play should remove reuse. Preflight failure, immediate boss repetition, or repeating one concept while a sibling is unseen is not acceptable reuse.

## Pool expectations

- **Bosses:** three unique prompts per encounter; family pools should have nine per tier and rotate concept, skill, and scenario.
- **Repair:** one-error scaffold, easier than the missed task, specific misconception, same-skill route, then retest. Repair Seed is optional runtime support but the current all-zero Macro inventory requires a policy decision.
- **Bridge:** source and destination skills must both be recoverable from metadata or stem; same-skill retests labeled Bridge do not qualify.
- **Calculation:** breadth of task families outranks count. State precision/rounding and units when non-exact. Reject number swaps without a new reasoning demand.
- **Graphs:** required by the prompt, sufficient to answer, readable, unambiguous, described accessibly, and not repeated with the same cognitive task until monotonous.
- **Integration:** requires two or more causal/model links. It is synthesis, not a dumping ground for difficult one-topic items.

## Readiness vocabulary

ROBUST = ample margin and representation; ADEQUATE = one clean unique intended run; THIN = launchable but shallow/imbalanced; NOT READY = preflight failure. These labels apply first to the family, then separately to granular solo safety.
