## Calculation convention and calibration

Only Q10–Q15, Q26 and Q29 govern content alignment. The whole exam was inspected for style; no excluded Foundations, Market Policy/Welfare or Trade question supplies a coverage requirement. This artifact does not reproduce exam stems, tables or an answer key.

The bank expressly teaches midpoint calculation for endpoint schedules and also contains ratio-of-given-percentage exercises. The selected exam's multiple-choice calculation appears to use starting-value percentages. These are different conventions. Preserve the bank's explicit midpoint curriculum; name the method on revised endpoint items, retain the stated base for given percentages, and confirm revenue changes directly with P×Q. Do not silently replace midpoint with initial-value percentages or vice versa. A finite-change revenue claim must be checked directly, especially near unit elasticity.

Q10/Q12 motivate determinant-to-graph inference rather than merely naming a curve. Q11 motivates imbalance size and adjustment without a legal price restriction. Q13/Q14 motivate sign and elasticity determinants in context. Q15 motivates clean computation. Q26 motivates reverse simultaneous shifts with valid determinant pairs and explicit ambiguity. Q29 motivates using a calculation to advise on revenue, without claiming a global optimum from an incomplete schedule.
