# Phase 3A - Official Asset Inventory

All records below are existing approved production WebPs. Source files were referenced in place and were not copied, renamed, resized, re-encoded, or edited. Background and mode-card images are decorative at runtime; guide, boss, and artifact labels provide meaningful accessible names.

The per-asset tables provide asset ID, filename, compatible slots, dimensions, origin, and accessibility label. The shared metadata below completes the normalized inventory fields without repeating identical values on every row.

## Exclusions

- Economics question graphs, including Phase 2A Market Gate graph WebPs, are excluded from this library.
- Streak badges, achievement art, hub thumbnails, music, ambience, and sound effects are not exposed as Phase 3A theme slots.
- Archives, dated backups, snapshots, and duplicate fixed copies were not used as sources.
- Market Citadel and National Ledger have no dedicated approved Quiz, Trial by Graph, Fading Fortune, or Risk & Reward cards; the generated shell uses intentional mode-symbol fallbacks.
