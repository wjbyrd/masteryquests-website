# The Labyrinth of Choice — Trial by Graph Backlog

This is the deferred graph-content phase. Engine 2.0 is already structured so Trial by Graph can be enabled after the graph assets and graph-dependent questions are added.

Recommended first graph set:

1. Budget constraint — baseline intercepts and slope
2. Budget constraint — income increase/decrease parallel shifts
3. Budget constraint — X-price decrease/increase rotations
4. Indifference map — higher/lower utility and non-crossing curves
5. Consumer optimum — budget/indifference tangency and MRS = price ratio
6. Income and substitution effects — decomposition of an own-price change
7. Individual demand — optimal quantity at multiple own prices
8. Demand shift vs. movement along demand
9. Consumer surplus — area under demand above price
10. Market demand — horizontal summation of individual demands
11. Linear demand — elastic, unit-elastic, and inelastic regions with total revenue
12. Elastic vs. inelastic demand comparison
13. Perfectly elastic vs. perfectly inelastic limiting cases
14. Income change — normal vs. inferior-good demand responses
15. Related-good price change — substitutes vs. complements

For Trial by Graph, each authored item should eventually carry:

- `image`
- `imageAlt`
- `graphDescription`
- `graphRequired: true`
- explicit `objective`
- explicit `primarySkill`
- a question that cannot be answered correctly without actually using the graph

Target before enabling the mode: at least 20 strong non-boss graph questions, ideally 30+ so 10-, 15-, and 20-question runs have room to rotate without obvious repetition.
