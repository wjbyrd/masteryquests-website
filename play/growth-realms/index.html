CHOOSE YOUR ECONOMY

Field Guide

Commit to build, inspect both allocations and results, then begin a new cycle. Output is production during a cycle; the other stocks carry forward. Labor grows automatically. Focus on output per worker, a proxy for living standards.

The rival follows a randomly selected doctrine and its own economic conditions. Its plan is revealed after resolution; its doctrine is revealed at the end. The economy is deterministic for a given history. Your run stays in this tab; reloading starts over.

YOUR SIX-CYCLE CHALLENGE

Build. Compare. Adapt.

  1. Choose your starting challenge. Catch up as Rivermark or sustain Meridian’s lead.
  2. Invest 20 points each cycle. Click a district for +1. Use the controls below your city to add, undo, or clear points.
  3. Commit, then compare. Watch both cities develop. Review productivity and constraints before your next move.

After six cycles, trace your growth path and uncover the rival’s strategy. Replay to test a different investment mix against a newly selected rival doctrine.
