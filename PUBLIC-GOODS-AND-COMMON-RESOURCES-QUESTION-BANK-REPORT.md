## Representative records

### Efficient provision

ID 42160:

> Refer to the emergency-sirens graph. At what quantity do MSB and MC intersect?

Answer: `40 sirens`

Feedback: MSB intersects MC at 40 sirens, so the marginal social benefit of the 40th siren equals its marginal cost.

### Free riding and underprovision

ID 42173:

> What problem is illustrated when Private Benefit supports 40 hours but MSB supports 80 hours?

Answer: `Free riding causes revealed private benefit to understate social benefit`

Feedback explains that nonexcludability weakens payment/revelation incentives, causing the private-benefit curve to understate MSB and provision to fall below 80 hours.

### Context-dependent classification

ID 42214:

> A streaming service blocks nonmembers, and the platform has ample capacity so one more viewer does not degrade service. What is it?

Answer: `Club good`

Feedback identifies both dimensions: subscription access is excludable and spare-capacity viewing is nonrival over the stated range.

### Private provision

ID 42272:

> Why might a donation-funded flood barrier be smaller than the efficient barrier?

Answer: `Individual donations may not capture benefits received by noncontributors`

Feedback connects noncontributors' protection to voluntary revenue understating total social willingness to pay.

### Property rights and incentives

ID 42308:

> A toll is introduced on a congested road. How can this improve resource-use incentives?

Answer: `Drivers face a price connected to scarce road capacity and delay`

Feedback explains that the toll creates practical exclusion and makes users consider more of the congestion cost.
