Economics in Play

Run a food truck, manage a concert, or build an investment portfolio. These short games layer economics into the choices you make and the consequences you see. Play for fun, experiment, and connect what happens to what we are learning in class.

Getting Started

  1. Pick a game for this week. Use the guide below or explore the game hub.
  2. Try a short play session. Make choices, notice the tradeoffs, and see how the results change. Play at your own pace and replay if you want.
  3. Tell us what you think. These games are in beta, so your feedback can help improve the experience.
