# Light-Touch Supporting Bank Standard

## Purpose

A supporting bank gives a lightly taught concept strong application, remediation, checkpoint, and mixed-play coverage without manufacturing full standalone depth.

## Evidence-based profile

1. Easy, Medium, Hard: target about 8 each; never below engine floors.
2. Elite: 3–4 authentic transfer items.
3. Legendary: normally 10–14; do not force 27.
4. Ordinary bosses: at least 3 per checkpoint tier.
5. Legendary Boss: 4–6 with role rotation.
6. Repair: at least 6 one-error diagnostics covering major misconceptions.
7. Bridge: at least 4 explicit connections to other canonical skills.
8. Ordinary pools should be application-led; concentrate definitions in Repair and Bridge.
9. Reject exact, material near, answer-set, scenario-template, and repeated-cognitive-task filler.
10. No pool may exceed 50% uniquely-longest correct; balance answer positions without mechanical uniformity.

## Runtime expectations

Solo ordinary modes must start and complete normally. Solo Legendary may reuse only after all eligible ordinary items are exhausted; recent suppression must prevent immediate or short-window loops. Mixed play is primary: the concept must remain represented, not dominate, and complete all modes.

## When full standalone is required

Use full standalone status when the concept receives substantial instruction, has many authentic task families, must sustain unique solo Legendary play, or is a central prerequisite repeatedly practiced on its own.

## Composer recommendation

Do not add production bank-type metadata from one pilot. After multiple supporting pilots confirm the profile, add validated metadata and a user-facing note that solo Legendary uses controlled reuse.
