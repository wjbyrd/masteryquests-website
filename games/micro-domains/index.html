Adaptive engine

The mode changes what the engine does with student evidence.

Mastery Quests does not use one hidden adaptive rule for every activity. Full-evidence modes, Legendary Mode, Quiz, and the short game modes use different selection and reporting rules because they produce different amounts and kinds of evidence.

Full adaptive + remediation

Standard, Timed, Exam Drill, Score Attack, Unlimited

Question selection responds to performance, coverage, difficulty readiness, skill and concept weakness, and repetition control. When the evidence supports it, the engine can leave the normal stream for targeted Repair, Bridge, and Retest work before returning to regular play.

Adaptive challenge

Legendary Mode

Legendary remains adaptive, but its purpose is pressure-testing rather than remediation. It favors the hardest available material and does not interrupt the challenge with repair detours.

Limited-run evidence

Quiz, Fading Fortune, Risk & Reward, Trial by Graph

Quiz samples broadly without chasing weakness. Fading Fortune, Risk & Reward, and Trial by Graph begin with broad calibration and then investigate emerging signals inside their eligible question pool. These modes do not use repair detours, and their reports remain sample-aware.

Collection note: The Labyrinth of Choice uses the current limited-run adaptive standard, including Trial by Graph. Future Micro domains will expose graph-only practice only when their rebuilt banks support it.
