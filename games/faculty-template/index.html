What the template includes

A complete engine with an empty instructional layer

01

Ten game modes

Standard Campaign, Timed Trial, Exam Drill, Quiz, Unlimited Practice, Legendary Mode, Score Attack, Trial by Graph, Fading Fortune, and Risk & Reward are available when the question bank supports them.

02

Adaptive support

Guide introductions, rotating Daily Tasks, repair and bridge routing, cinematic checkpoints, usable artifact powers, progress saving, and multi-signal mastery reporting remain intact.

03

Safe editing regions

Faculty question banks and generated configuration are separated from the protected engine.

04

Neutral presentation

The template does not require source-game artwork, audio, lore, or instructional-resource files.

Manual workflow

Author carefully and validate before publishing

  1. 01

    Define the subject and objectives

    Determine the learning scope, mode requirements, and expected mastery evidence.

  2. 02

    Create the question architecture

    Build main, boss, repair, bridge, and legendary pools with stable IDs and skill routes.

  3. 03

    Insert content only in marked regions

    Preserve protected engine constants, IDs, state variables, and mode functions.

  4. 04

    Validate and simulate

    Audit answers, duplicates, routing, bosses, saving, timers, reports, downloads, and mobile behavior.
