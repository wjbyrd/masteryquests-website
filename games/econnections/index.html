ECON-NECTIONS

Daily Economic Puzzle

Find the four economic connections.

16 tiles. Four hidden groups. Three strikes.
Choose your daily puzzle and connect what you know.

A streak grows when you solve either puzzle each day. Solving both counts as one day.

How to play
  1. Select four tiles that share an economic connection.
  2. Submit to check your group. Correct connections lock in with an explanation.
  3. Find all four groups before your third strike. On strike three, the remaining connections are revealed.

Connections may be concepts, cause-and-effect sequences, or common misconceptions. Tile order within a group does not matter. Repeating the same wrong set does not cost another strike.

Group difficulty runs from 1 (light teal) to 4 (deep blue). Your progress is saved in this browser. No account needed.
