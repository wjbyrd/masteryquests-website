Subscribe by email

Confirm, unsubscribe, or request removal

When sign-up is available, Brevo sends a confirmation link. You join the update list only after confirming your address. Repeat sign-ups do not create duplicate subscriptions. Previously unsubscribed addresses are not automatically re-enrolled; contact the creator if you want help subscribing again.

Use the unsubscribe link in any update email to stop future updates. You can also email wjbyrd@troy.edu to unsubscribe or request removal of your contact record.

Brevo holds your email and subscription preferences for this list, separately from gameplay and classroom participation records. Active subscriptions remain until you unsubscribe or request removal. After unsubscribe, a suppression record may remain to prevent accidental re-enrollment. Read the communications privacy notice.
