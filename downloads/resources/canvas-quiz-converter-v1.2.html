3Review

REQUIRED RULES:
1. Return only the finished quiz text. Do not add an introduction, explanation, markdown fence, or closing note.
2. Begin with Quiz Title: and Quiz Description:.
3. Every question must begin with Title:, Type:, and Points:, followed by a sequentially numbered stem.
4. Use only these exact Type values: Multiple Choice, True/False, Multiple Answers, Fill in the Blank, or Essay.
5. Multiple Choice questions must contain four choices labeled A) through D) and exactly one starred correct answer.
6. True/False questions must use Answer: True or Answer: False. Do not add A) and B) choices.
7. Multiple Answers questions must contain four choices labeled A) through D), at least two starred correct answers, and at least one incorrect answer.
8. Fill in the Blank questions must contain one or more Accepted Answer: lines and a Case Sensitive: Yes or No line.
9. Essay questions must contain General Feedback: and must not contain answer choices or accepted answers.
10. Every auto-graded question must contain Correct Feedback: and Incorrect Feedback:.
11. Keep feedback accurate and instructional. Do not reveal the answer in the question stem.
12. Verify that every question assesses one of the supplied learning objectives and that exactly one question block exists for each requested question.
13. Use plain text. Preserve every label, colon, answer label, and asterisk exactly.
