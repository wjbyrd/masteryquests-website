# Faculty Composition Contract — Phase 4.4

Version 1.1.0

Every selected concept is assigned exactly once to Stage One, Stage Two, or Stage Three. Stage assignment controls checkpoint boss sourcing only: Stage One supplies `easyBoss`, Stage Two supplies `mediumBoss`, and Stage Three supplies `finalBoss`. Ordinary questions from every selected concept remain available throughout the game according to canonical difficulty.

Generation is mode-aware. Only selected modes impose blocking minimums. An Exam-only or Timed-and-Exam composition does not require checkpoint boss pools. Legendary-only requires six legendary and three legendary-boss items. Standard and Score Attack require all three checkpoint boss pools.

Question objects, option order, numeric answers, published hashes, routing metadata, and instructional roles are preserved. Direct repair routes take precedence over general repair pools. Conflicting objective labels and duplicate canonical IDs block generation.
