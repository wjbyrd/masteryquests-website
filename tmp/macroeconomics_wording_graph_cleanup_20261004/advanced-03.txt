PM2B2-INDEX-L-003 legendary
A threshold is $40,000 at CPI 100. The contract adjusts next year using the previous year’s inflation. CPI rises to 110, then 121; by the second year the threshold has received only the first 10% adjustment. What is the shortfall from full current indexation?
A: $4,000; apply the second 10% only to the original threshold
B: $8,400; the threshold never received any adjustment
C: $4,400; the paid threshold is $44,000 instead of $48,400
D: $0; a one-year lag cannot alter purchasing power
Full current indexation requires 40,000 × 121/100 = 48,400. The lagged threshold is 40,000 × 1.10 = 44,000. The gap is 4,400; successive inflation rates apply to changing price levels.
UPGRADE Preserve purchasing-power/indexation reasoning; require evaluating a lagged contract against a counterfactual household basket or identifying a missing assumption.
