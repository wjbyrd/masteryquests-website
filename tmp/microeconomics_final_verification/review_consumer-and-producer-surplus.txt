A buyer values a unit at $36, its production cost is $21 and its price is $27. A delivery service would add $5 to buyer value and use $3 of real resources; its $4 fee is paid by the buyer to the service. Which complete change follows if the same trade occurs?
A:Total surplus rises from $15 to $20 because the delivery resource cost is a transfer.
B:Total surplus rises from $15 to $17; the buyer gains $1, the service gains $1, and the original seller’s $6 surplus is unchanged. [KEY]
C:Total surplus falls to $13 because the $4 fee and $3 resource cost should both be subtracted from the $5 benefit.
D:Total surplus stays $15 because a new service can only redistribute the original gains.
Feedback: Original CS=$9 and PS=$6. Delivery raises buyer value by $5 at a $4 fee, so the buyer gains $1. The service receives $4 and uses $3, gaining $1. Net new surplus is $5−$3=$2.
