    How to read this report

    Results cover the questions in these files. Different runs can contain different questions. Revised questions appear separately. Older files without saved question details are counted as unavailable.

    First exposure means no earlier answer or rapid guess was retained for that question in the run. Short or incomplete records may miss earlier attempts. Assistance reflects recorded choice removal. Outside help is unknown.

    Answers with matching attempt identifiers are counted once across overlapping files. Repeated attempts remain counted. Files without reliable identifiers carry a warning because their overlap cannot be verified. Rapid guesses and unfinished exam selections are excluded from accuracy.

    The review threshold changes the Questions to Review list. Every response remains in the totals and question details. Copyable feedback requires at least five responses across two runs for a comparison. Runs may include repeated practice by one student.
